Our Story

Golden Aura began with a single variegated Monstera on a sunny windowsill and a love for plants that feel like living art.

Today we hand-select rare and exotic plants from trusted growers, nurture them in our greenhouse and send them to your door healthy, happy and ready to thrive.

Every plant comes with care guidance so your collection keeps growing for years.
