Plant Care Tips

Keep your green friends thriving

☀️

Light

Most exotic aroids love bright, indirect light. Avoid harsh midday sun which scorches variegated leaves.

💧

Water

Water when the top 2–3 cm of soil feels dry. Always use pots with drainage holes.

🌡️

Humidity

Aim for 50–60% humidity. Group plants together or use a pebble tray in dry rooms.

🪴

Soil & Feeding

Use a chunky, airy mix and feed with balanced fertiliser monthly in the growing season.
